Shape Guide

Camber, rocker and everything between

The shape under your feet changes how a ski or board behaves far more than the graphics on top of it.

Groomers

Traditional camber

Arched underfoot with contact points near tip and tail. Maximum spring out of a turn and the best grip you can get on ice, at the cost of being demanding in soft snow.

All mountain

Early rise hybrid

Camber underfoot with a lifted tip and tail. Turns start easily, chopped snow feels calmer, and nothing about it punishes a mistake. The safe answer for most riders.

Deep snow

Full rocker

Reverse camber that keeps the tips planing without you leaning back. Effortless in bowls and trees, vaguer on hardpack, and much kinder to your legs on a storm day.

Snowboard bindings

Step On or straps, your choice at booking

Both systems are in the fleet in every size, and swapping between them mid-week costs nothing.

  • Step On: three locking points at toe and heel. You clip in standing up, off the chairlift, in a few seconds.
  • Ratchet straps: ankle and toe straps you can tune to the millimetre, which suits riders who like a specific feel.
  • Highbacks and disc angles are set to your stance before the board goes into the locker.
