Our Method

How teaching works here

Small groups, honest feedback and a clear idea of what you are working on by the end of the session.

Five riders per coach

Never more, even in peak weeks. Groups are sorted by ability on the first run, not by booking order.

Video you keep

Two clips per session, sent to your phone with the coach's notes written underneath.

Gear checked first

Coaches look at your boots and edges before the first run, because bad equipment looks like bad technique.

One clear takeaway

You leave with a single thing to practise, not a list of ten you will forget by the car park.

Progression

What comes next for you

A rough map of the skills that unlock each part of the mountain.

Green runs

Wedge into first parallel

Steering each leg on its own, keeping your weight over the middle of the ski, and coming to a stop wherever you choose rather than wherever the hill allows.

Blue cruisers

Rhythm and pole timing

Rolling the edges instead of pushing the tails, side-slipping an icy patch calmly, and holding a rhythm down a whole pitch without stopping.

Black and bowls

Steep hold and deep snow

Carrying speed through a carved turn, hop turns in a narrow line, and staying balanced when the snow is soft enough to swallow a tip.

Safety Check

The kit check before every backcountry day

Your guide runs through this list at the summit gate with each rider individually. No exceptions, including for guides.

Transceiver — switched to send, battery above 90 percent, and a search check inside 40 metres before anyone leaves the gate.
Probe — 280cm, assembled once so you know the cord locks on the first throw, with depth markings readable.
Metal shovel — blade locked onto the shaft and stowed in the dedicated pocket, not buried under a jacket at the bottom of the pack.
The morning bulletin — read together with the guide, with the day's problem, the loaded aspects and the turnaround time agreed out loud.

Safety equipment is included with every guided day. If you own your own, bring it and we will check it alongside ours.
