Cherrapunji, Meghalaya

About The Place

Known locally as Sohra — one of the wettest places on Earth, where waterfalls, living root bridges, and limestone caves come alive in the monsoon.Cherrapunji sits on the southern edge of the Khasi Hills in Meghalaya, famously nicknamed the "Rain Capital of the World" for the staggering rainfall it receives each monsoon. The landscape is a mix of deep gorges, plunging waterfalls, and dense forest, shaped over centuries by the region's unique wet-and-dry seasonal rhythm.The area is also home to the Khasi people's remarkable living root bridges — footbridges grown over decades by training the roots of rubber fig trees across rivers — a form of bio-engineering found nowhere else in the world.

Nickname: Rain Capital of the World
Elevation: ~1,484 metres
Best time to visit: June – September (monsoon)
Nearest airport: Shillong / Guwahati

Why Visit Cherrapunji?

  • Walk across the Double Decker Living Root Bridge, a two-tiered natural marvel.

  • See Nohkalikai Falls, India's third-tallest waterfall, thunder at full flow.

  • Explore the limestone chambers of Mawsmai Cave.

  • Take in sweeping views from Mawkdok Dympep Valley Viewpoint.

The best overall time to visit Meghalaya is from October to April, offering pleasant weather (4°C to 25°C), clear skies, and smooth travel conditions for sightseeing, trekking, and crystal-clear river views.
