Getting to the base station

Finding us on arrival day

The depot is on the plaza level directly beneath the gondola, signposted from the main car park and the bus turning circle.

  • Free two-hour parking in plaza level P1 while you get fitted
  • Valley shuttle stops outside the door every 20 minutes
  • Ski-in access from run 3, racks outside the west entrance
  • Step-free entrance and a ground floor fitting room

Left something behind?

Lost property, found gear and forgotten gloves

Anything handed in at a depot is logged the same day and kept for 90 days. Tell us roughly where and when, and we will look through the shelf for you.

  • Items found on the snow go to patrol first, then to the base depot
  • We post anything found to your home address at cost
  • Phones and wallets are kept in the safe, collected with photo ID
