Styling Philosophy

The Three Ensembles Protocol

True sartorial distinction lies in pairing the correct weight of worsted with matching neckwear and hallmarked jewelry.

1. The Boardroom Command

Navy worsted two-piece matched with 7-fold ancient madder silk neckties, white poplin shirt, and dark cognac English bridle leather.

2. The Ceremonial Protocol

Midnight barathea dinner suit paired with a 7cm grosgrain self-tie butterfly bow tie, forged onyx cufflinks, and patent calf oxfords.

3. The Country Sporting

Heavyweight Cheviot tweed coat paired with high-twist cavalry twill trousers, knitted silk-cashmere neckwear, and Goodyear-welted brogues.

Micro-Craftsmanship

The Finishes That Define SARTOR

The details invisible to the casual eye that separate authentic bespoke from commercial production.

Barchetta Breast Pocket

A hand-cut, gently boat-shaped pocket curve following the natural swell of the chest blade, cut cleanly across the chest canvas.

Cross-Stitched Horn Shanks

Buttons carved from Scottish stag and natural water buffalo horn, secured with thick waxed thread cross-stitches that resist tearing.

Fish-Eye Trousers Darts

Concave double darts tailored along the lower back waistband that sculpt the fabric into the hollow of the spine without gathering.
