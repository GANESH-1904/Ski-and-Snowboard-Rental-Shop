Anatomy Calibration

Anatomy-Specific Engineering

How our individual paper patterns neutralize posture challenges.

Dropped Shoulder Compensation

Seventy-eight percent of clients exhibit a dominant right shoulder drop. Off-the-peg jackets break diagonally across the blade. SARTOR lowers the right armscye gorge by up to 8mm, creating perfect level symmetry at the hemline.

Forward Neck & Head Pitch

Prolonged digital desk posture draws the head forward, creating a hollow 2-inch gap behind the jacket collar. We re-hang the collar pitch with an advanced forward curve, locking the jacket flush against your neck band.

Commission Timing

Fitting Milestones & Craft Schedules

From raw measurements to your final daylight handover in Mayfair.

01

Consultation & Baste

Weeks 1 – 3: Paper pattern carving, initial cloth cutting, and basting together of the skeleton garment with white cotton thread.

02

Forward Fitting

Weeks 4 – 6: Canvases pad-stitched into chest, shoulder slope set, buttonholes cut, and sleeve pitch confirmed.

03

Handover & Pressing

Weeks 7 – 9: Hand-pressing on vintage steam bucks, final daylight inspection, and handover in custom cedar garment case.
