Bootfitting lab

Sore feet end a ski day faster than bad snow

Most of the tiredness riders blame on fitness comes from a boot that does not match the shape of the foot. Every reservation includes a session on our pressure-scan bench, and adjustments are free for the length of your rental.

  • Infrared foot scan accurate to half a millimetre
  • Heat-moulded liners and cork footbeds shaped to your arch
  • Shell punching for wide forefeet and tender ankle bones
  • Swap to another shell at any depot if something still rubs

Safety First

What we check before gear leaves the depot

Every ski, board and helmet passes the same checklist, whether it is going out for a day or a fortnight.

Helmets with rotational protection

The whole helmet fleet uses MIPS liners, and any helmet that takes a hard knock is retired the same day.

Bindings on the test bench

Release values are measured on a calibrated Montana bench against the ISO standard, not set by eye.

Boots and helmets cleaned

Everything that touches skin runs through an ozone cabinet between riders, then dries in the pod overnight.
