A Day With Us

How a concierge day runs

A typical Tuesday in February, from the first message to the last pair of skis collected.

06:30

Snow note from your technician

A short message with the overnight total, wind on the summit and which pair of skis is waiting by the door.

08:45

Gear ready in the ski room

Boots warmed, skis waxed, goggles wiped. Your guide meets you at the lift rather than the village.

12:30

Midday swap if the weather turns

Message the technician from the chairlift and a different setup is waiting at the mid-mountain pod after lunch.

16:15

Valet collection

Drop everything at the cabana, change into your shoes and walk home. The workshop handles the rest overnight.
