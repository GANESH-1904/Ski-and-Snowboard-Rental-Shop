Damage Waiver

Should you take the damage waiver?

Four dollars a day. Here is exactly what changes if you do.

With the waiver
  • Base gouges and core repairs covered in full
  • Edge damage from rocks covered in full
  • Bent or broken poles replaced at no charge
  • No excess and no assessment argument at return
Without it, at workshop rates
  • Base repair from $65
  • Edge section replacement $120
  • Pole replacement $45
  • A ski written off by a core shot, up to $750

Normal wear, scratches and edge dulling are never charged, waiver or not.
